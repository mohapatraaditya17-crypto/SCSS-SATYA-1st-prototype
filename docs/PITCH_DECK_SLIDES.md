### Slide 3: The Innovation: 4-Layer Defensible Architecture

**Headline:** Defensible 4-Layer Forensic Architecture  

- **Layer 1: Passive-Blind Multi-Signal Screening (9 Forensic Engines):**
  - *Metadata & Deep PNG Chunk Forensics:* IHDR bit depth, pHYs display DPI, creation timestamps, and trailing-byte steganography detection.
  - *Error Level Analysis (ELA):* JPEG 8×8 DCT macroblock recompression variance analysis.
  - *Multi-Scale Noise Residual Analysis:* High-pass median filter local variance mapping across all formats.
  - *2D Fast Fourier Spectral Analysis:* 2D FFT magnitude spectrum to identify periodic resampling lattices and generative AI grid spikes.
  - *Copy-Move Forgery Detection:* ORB keypoint descriptor matching with spatial separation to reveal duplicated content.
  - *AI Generative Classifier:* Calibrated spectral power decay slope ($\alpha$) and cross-channel noise co-occurrence to detect diffusion models (Midjourney, DALL-E).
  - *Document Typography & Alignment Engine:* Character glyph baseline offset and edge gradient blur divergence to detect altered numbers, dates, and text.
- **Layer 2: Cryptographic Evidence Sealing:**
  - Instant SHA-256 baseline hashing at ingestion; bitstream preserved in an isolated evidence vault with real-time audit re-verification.
- **Layer 3: Explainable Findings & Scientific Limits:**
  - Transparent checklist (*No Indicator / Indicator / Inconclusive*), quantitative analytics (SNR, Kurtosis, Fourier decay $\alpha$), mandatory scientific limitations, and manual verification steps.
- **Layer 4: Qualified Human Forensic Certification:**
  - Formal examiner attestation workflow generating court-admissible ISO/IEC 27037 PDF reports with two-pass dynamic page counts and embedded derived exhibits.

> **Speaker Note:**
> *"SCSS SATYA is built on a 4-layer architecture. Layer 1 doesn't rely on a single trick—it combines 9 multi-signal tests: noise residual heatmaps, 2D Fourier frequency grids, copy-move clone vectors, and document typography alignment. Layer 2 seals the file with a SHA-256 hash. Layer 3 explains every single finding with exact numbers and limitations. Layer 4 allows a forensic examiner to review, attest, and generate an ISO/IEC 27037 compliant court-ready report."*
